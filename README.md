**Velvet Bay** is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in your
browser. You are Rio Marlowe, a wheelman fresh out of the docks, taking jobs
from fixer Delia Voss and Marta Quill across a sun-soaked coastal city of
towers, traffic, beaches and a long pier.

Explore on foot or by car — steal any parked ride with **E** and drive it.
The Bay PD watches: theft, assault and hit-and-runs raise your wanted level
from one star to a citywide manhunt, with cruisers chasing and ramming you,
foot cops moving in to arrest you, roadblocks at 4 stars and the CONDOR-1
helicopter overhead at 3. Run 8 story jobs — including the timed cross-city
run **Harbor Lights**, the stealth escort **Quiet Cargo** (any wanted star
fails the deal) and the finale **The Velvet Run** — fight back with 4 weapons
and hitscan gunplay, hunt 12 hidden packages ($150 each + $2,500 bonus), and
build your bankroll. A live minimap tracks you, traffic, police and your next
objective; progress autosaves to localStorage (SAVE_VERSION 2).

Everything is original and self-contained: a procedural three.js city with
extruded curved cars, capsule characters, textured facades and cinematic
golden-hour / night lighting, a synthesized WebAudio soundtrack and sound
effects, and no network requests at all once the page loads.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080> in desktop Chrome.

**Controls:** W A S D move · SHIFT run · E enter / exit car / start job ·
MOUSE drag to look · WHEEL zoom · CLICK / F shoot · 1-4 weapons ·
SPACE handbrake · P pause · M music · R retry mission
