**Velvet Bay** is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in your
browser. You are Rio Marlowe, a wheelman fresh out of the docks, taking jobs
from fixer Delia Voss across a sun-soaked coastal city of towers, traffic,
beaches and a long pier.

Explore on foot or by car — steal any parked ride with **E** and drive it.
The Bay PD watches: theft, assault and hit-and-runs raise your wanted level
from one star to a citywide manhunt, with cruisers chasing you and foot cops
moving in to arrest you. Run three story missions (steal a getaway car,
deliver a package to the pier against the clock, take back a shipment from
the Red Tide gang at the dockyard), fight back with hitscan gunplay, grab
street cash, and build your bankroll. A live minimap tracks you, traffic,
police and your next objective; progress autosaves to localStorage.

Everything is original and self-contained: a procedural three.js city, a
synthesized WebAudio soundtrack and sound effects, and no network requests
at all once the page loads.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080> in desktop Chrome.

**Controls:** W A S D move · SHIFT run · E enter / exit car · MOUSE drag to
look · CLICK / F shoot · SPACE handbrake · P pause · M music · R retry mission
